# BeamNG Repository listing

Copy-paste for the upload form at beamng.com/resources. Upload
`dist/dotdash.zip` from the GitHub release (built with `--public`: no fonts
inside, it uses the game's own).

**Title**

    DotDash - minimal sport UI apps (tacho, boost, drivetrain start/stop, pedals)

**Category:** UI Apps

**Tag line** (one sentence)

    Thirteen clean black-glass UI apps: a line-and-needle tacho, boost, pedals, G meter, timers, and a start/stop button with your car's drivetrain controls.

**Description**

    DotDash is a set of minimal, sporty UI apps - black glass, white and one red.
    Use them one by one, sized and placed however you like, or all on one card.

    [B]DotTacho[/B] - rpm and speed in one round gauge. The revs are one thick line
    that fades in from idle with a short needle where the revs are; the redline
    lights up as you reach it and the line flashes at the shift point. Gear, turn
    signals, ABS / TCS / handbrake / high beam, water and fuel.
    [B]DotBoost[/B] - turbo pressure, vacuum to boost, with a peak hold.
    [B]DotStartStop[/B] - click to toggle the ignition, hold to crank; round it, the
    drivetrain controls your car really has: diff locks, low / high range,
    2WD / 4WD, hubs, ESC, drive modes, N2O, JATO.
    [B]DotRpm, DotGearRing, DotGear, DotGears[/B] - the revs as a bar, the gear in a
    rev ring, just the gear, or every gear of the box on one row.
    [B]DotPedals / DotPedalsClear[/B] - clutch, brake, throttle, handbrake 0-100 %,
    with or without a background.
    [B]DotGMeter, DotInfo, DotTimer[/B] - G with trail and peak; water, oil, fuel;
    0-100 / 0-200 km/h (0-60 / 0-120 mph) with your session best.
    [B]DotDash[/B] - everything on one card.

    Units follow your game options (km/h / mph, bar / kPa / psi, °C / °F).
    Each app is a single canvas redrawn only when new data arrives - light on
    the UI.

    [B]Install:[/B] put the zip in your mods folder, then UI Apps -> search "Dot".

    Source and issues: https://github.com/Tudor077/beamng-dotdash
